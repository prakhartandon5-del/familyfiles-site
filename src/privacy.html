familyfiles is built so that the information you enter about your family stays with you. This page explains what that means in practice, and what little information this website itself handles.

The familyfiles application

familyfiles is a downloadable application that runs in your web browser, on your own device. It does not require an account and there is no sign-in.

  • The information you enter is saved by your browser, on the device you are using.
  • It is not uploaded to us, and we have no ability to read it.
  • There is no server-side database of customer family information.
  • The application works with your internet connection switched off.
  • Backups and exported files are created locally and saved wherever you choose. You control who receives them.

Because your information is stored by your browser, clearing your browser’s site data can remove it. The application includes an export option so you can keep your own backup.

familyfiles deliberately does not ask for passwords, PINs, Social Security numbers or complete account numbers. It asks what an account is and where the real record is kept.

This website

This website is a single static page. It does not set advertising cookies, does not run third-party tracking scripts, and does not ask you to submit any personal information.

As with any website, our hosting provider (Vercel) processes standard technical request data such as IP address and browser type in order to serve the page and protect against abuse. We do not use this to identify you.

Purchases

Payments are processed by our payment provider, not by us. When you buy familyfiles, the payment provider collects the information needed to take payment and deliver your download — typically your email address and billing details — and handles it under its own privacy policy. We receive your email address so we can deliver the product and respond if you contact us about your order. We do not receive your full card details.

Your choices

You can delete everything the application has stored at any time using Reset inside familyfiles, or by clearing site data in your browser. If you would like us to delete the order information associated with your purchase, contact us and we will do so where we are not required to keep it for accounting purposes.
